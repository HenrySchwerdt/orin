- **Interruptible network waits and operation-wide deadlines (ADR-0017 Addendum D, C3).** A task blocked in `std:net` accept, connect, recv or send wakes within milliseconds with the new error kind `Cancelled` when its scope is cancelled from another task (`Scope.cancel()`, or a copyable `CancelToken` from `Scope.token()`; `isCancelled()` is the cooperative check); the platform layer polls a per-scope wake source (an eventfd, generation-tagged handles) together with the socket (1,163 of 1,400 L0 lines). Every net operation also has an absolute `Deadline` form (`recvUntil`, `sendAllUntil`, `readExactUntil`, `withAcceptedUntil`, `withConnectionUntil`, ...) and a `connect` timeout now bounds all addresses of a name together. Limits: `getaddrinfo` stays blocking and uninterruptible, and waits on channels do not observe cancellation yet (C2).
