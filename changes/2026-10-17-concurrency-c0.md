- **Concurrency Gate 1, C0: cancellation and outcome design (ADR-0017 Addendum B, ADR-0053).** Accepts the structured-cancellation design (cancellation tree, task outcomes, exactly-once results, cooperative checkpoints, absolute monotonic deadlines, no new syntax) and adds its pure state machines to `std:taskcore` (`CancelState`, `CancelScope`, `TaskCell`, outcome lattice and deadline arithmetic, all with contracts and tests). Nothing blocks differently yet. ADR-0053 raises the L0 platform budget from 1,000 to 1,400 code lines (every addition needs contracts and a CBMC harness) for the timed and interruptible waits of C1/C3; `tools/verify_platform.sh` enforces the new limit. The roadmap records the owner defaults D1-D12 taken on 2026-10-16.
