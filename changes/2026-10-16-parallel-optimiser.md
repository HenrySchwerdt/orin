- **Parallel per-function optimiser (ADR-0049 row 15a).** `optimize` runs its per-function step (`optimizeFunction`) in pieces of 64 functions on the task pool, with the inputs `{ q, p }` lent through `mapChunksLent` and the pieces concatenated in function order; the program-wide analyses and the rewrites that follow them (`forwardProgram`, `dropUniqueProgram`, `uncheckProgram`) stay sequential, because lending them copies nearly the whole program back for little work. The generated C and LLVM output and all diagnostics are unchanged at every `ORIN_THREADS`. `tools/test_threads.py` checks the optimised MIR of 41 programs and the C and LLVM output of `compiler/main.or` for equality at 1, 2, 4 and 8 threads.
