- **Contract verifier: functions and types are qualified by their unit.** `orin verify --contracts` keyed free functions, classes, records and unions by their bare name, so every call of a name declared in more than one unit (`mk`, `isDigit`, `dirname`) and every use of a type name declared more than once (`Scope`, `Block`, `Workspace`) was UNKNOWN ("not a unique function", "declared more than once"). The verifier now keys them as `unit::name` and resolves each name in the scope of the unit it is written in (own declarations plus imports and re-exports, as the checker binds them; the callee's signature and a record's invariant are resolved in the declaring unit). A name bound twice in one scope, or not bound to a declaration of the program, still gives UNKNOWN. On the compiler's own `check`, `syntax`, `loader` and `package` modules the 48 "callee not unique" and 85 "duplicate type name" obligations are gone, with no new REFUTED. Regression test: `tests/contracts/names/`.
