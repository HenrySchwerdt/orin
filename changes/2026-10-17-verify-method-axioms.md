- **Contract verifier models more array and string methods (ADR-0053 Addendum A, group U8).** `orin verify --contracts` no longer gives up with "array method `pop` is not supported" and its relatives: `xs.pop().unwrap()` (as a statement or the initialiser of a `let` / assignment) runs the `pop` and reports the `unwrap` as an obligation, `find` / `findIndex` are modelled exactly, `sort`, `reduce`, `split` (with an obligation that the separator is not empty) and `indexOf` by sound partial axioms, and `startsWith` / `endsWith` / `includes` are now functions of their two strings (the same test in the body and in a contract agrees; length, empty, equality and prefix-implies-substring axioms; also allowed inside quantifier bodies) (`compiler/vc/methods.or`, documented in `docs/contracts.md`). Claims the axioms do not prove stay UNKNOWN, never REFUTED. The compiler's own `Fixpoint.take` (compiler/verify/verify.or) now states what its doc comment says (the worklist holds only block ids) as a class invariant and as a `@Requires` of the private method, which the verifier now reaches and had refuted for lack of it.
