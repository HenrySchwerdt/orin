- **Loop invariants on `for .. of` (ADR-0053 Addendum A).** An `@Invariant` in front of `for (const x of xs)` may use the read-only `usize` pseudo-variable `loopIndex`, the position in `xs` of the iteration it is about (0 on entry, `xs.length` after the loop); the verifier assumes `0 <= i < xs.length` and `x == xs[i]` in the body, proves the invariant on entry and for one iteration, and uses it after the loop. It is type-checked and erased like every loop invariant (a variable called `loopIndex` hides it: E0415); `for .. of` loops in `compiler/check` and `compiler/loader` gained invariants that turn UNKNOWN results into VERIFIED.
