- **Parallel MIR verifier (ADR-0049 row 14).** `verify` checks functions in pieces of 64 on the task pool, with the program lent read-only through `mapChunksLent` instead of copied; messages and their order are unchanged. The verify phase of `orin compile compiler/main.or` takes about 0.46x as long at `ORIN_THREADS=4` as at 1. `tools/test_threads.py` checks the C output, the verifier messages (hand-built and `mutate_mir.py` mutants) and `orin verify` for equality at 1, 2, 4 and 8 threads.
