- **`orin verify --contracts` solver budget (`--timeout-ms`, `--retry-ms`, `--rlimit`).** The z3 wall time of the first query is now configurable (default 4 s as before; `--timeout-ms 2000` halved the verify time of `compiler/opt` with no status change), the bounded counterexample search after an `unknown` is capped at 1 s (`--retry-ms`, 0 = off; it was 4 s and cost 45 % of the solver time for 26 counterexamples in 900 runs), and `--rlimit N` adds z3's deterministic resource limit. The hard limit follows the wall time. Documented in docs/contracts.md.
